Title
-----
Coframe rigidity and finite stationary separation for skew Schur functions

Keywords
--------
skew Schur function
Jacobi–Trudi coframe
fixed-cap rigidity
finite stationary identity
unit restoration
boundary reconstruction
determinant

Abstract (Plain)
----------------
The skew Schur function of a connected diagram, together with the function obtained by splicing the same fixed connected cap to it, determines the diagram up to translation. We prove this using complete rational Jacobi–Trudi coframes, retaining every unit and inverse pole. For zero-free diagrams and caps, a separate linearized theorem detects every nonzero constant-weight combination of vacancy restorations from the two observations. These results imply finite stationary separation: a shifted positive-power identity with a fixed rational potential forces two equal-function connected diagrams to coincide. Its precision is fixed by the supplied span and includes the terminal coefficient. We also treat a whole moving potential by field descent and prove an unshifted exclusion for opposite ends of one diagram. A weak-cap theorem recovers absolute flags, including units, from the complete rational coframe.
