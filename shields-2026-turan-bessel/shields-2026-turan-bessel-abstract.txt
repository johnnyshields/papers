Title
-----
Sharp coefficientwise positivity for a matrix Turán determinant of ₀F₁

Keywords
--------
coefficientwise positivity
Turán inequality
modified Bessel function
reciprocal-gamma series
hypergeometric function
order derivative
absolute monotonicity

Abstract (Plain)
----------------
Let Z(a,λ) = ₀F₁(;a;λ)/Γ(a) = ∑_{k≥0} λ^k/(k! Γ(a+k)) for a > 0. We prove a sharp coefficientwise positivity theorem for a confluent matrix Turán determinant of this reciprocal-gamma normalization. From parameter and Euler derivatives of Z, we form a one-parameter family of symmetric 2×2 matrices 𝒯_κ(a,λ) and prove that, for each fixed a > 0, every positive-degree Maclaurin coefficient of Δ^(κ) = det 𝒯_κ is strictly positive if and only if κ ≥ 1. Thus κ = 1 is the exact deformation threshold at every parameter value, and Δ^(κ)(a,·) is strictly absolutely monotone for every κ ≥ 1. At this endpoint, coefficientwise determinant positivity holds even though the first nonconstant coefficient matrix may be indefinite: a mixed-determinant polarization confines its possible negative contribution to the degree-two coefficient, whose positivity follows from an explicit decomposition.

Via the identity I_{a−1}(2√λ) = λ^((a−1)/2) Z(a,λ), an exact diagonal congruence transfers the endpoint theorem to the modified Bessel function I_ν, yielding, for every ν > −1 and z > 0, a positive-definite Bessel–Schur matrix coupling order, argument, and mixed order–argument derivatives of log I_ν. For each fixed ν, neither the deformation threshold κ = 1 nor the argument-independent correction 4/ψ₁(ν+1) can be lowered.

Abstract (Rich Text)
--------------------
Let \(Z(a,\lambda)={}_0F_1(;a;\lambda)/\Gamma(a)=\sum_{k\ge0}\lambda^k/(k!\,\Gamma(a+k))\) for \(a>0\). We prove a sharp coefficientwise positivity theorem for a confluent matrix Turán determinant of this reciprocal-gamma normalization. From parameter and Euler derivatives of \(Z\), we form a one-parameter family of symmetric \(2\times2\) matrices \(\mathcal T_\kappa(a,\lambda)\) and prove that, for each fixed \(a>0\), every positive-degree Maclaurin coefficient of \(\Delta^{(\kappa)}=\det\mathcal T_\kappa\) is strictly positive if and only if \(\kappa\ge1\). Thus \(\kappa=1\) is the exact deformation threshold at every parameter value, and \(\Delta^{(\kappa)}(a,\cdot)\) is strictly absolutely monotone for every \(\kappa\ge1\). At this endpoint, coefficientwise determinant positivity holds even though the first nonconstant coefficient matrix may be indefinite: a mixed-determinant polarization confines its possible negative contribution to the degree-two coefficient, whose positivity follows from an explicit decomposition.

Via the identity \(I_{a-1}(2\sqrt\lambda)=\lambda^{(a-1)/2}Z(a,\lambda)\), an exact diagonal congruence transfers the endpoint theorem to the modified Bessel function \(I_\nu\), yielding, for every \(\nu>-1\) and \(z>0\), a positive-definite Bessel–Schur matrix coupling order, argument, and mixed order–argument derivatives of \(\log I_\nu\). For each fixed \(\nu\), neither the deformation threshold \(\kappa=1\) nor the argument-independent correction \(4/\psi_1(\nu+1)\) can be lowered.
