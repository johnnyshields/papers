Title
-----
Fixed numerators in the Forgács–Tran family: bounded defect and angular zero discrepancy

Keywords
--------
real-rooted polynomial
rational generating function
exceptional zero
angular zero discrepancy
zero distribution
clock spacing
residue amplitude
initial conditions

Abstract (Plain)
----------------
Forgács and Tran proved that the polynomial sequence generated by the reciprocal of Q(t)+zt^r, with Q having only positive real zeros, is eventually real-rooted with all zeros in an explicit positive interval. Their Problem 14 asks what survives when a proper numerator, equivalently arbitrary fixed polynomial initial conditions, is introduced. No bound can be uniform over all numerators, but for each fixed numerator the number of zeros off the positive ray is bounded independently of the coefficient index, and eventually the same is true outside the Forgács–Tran interval. Restricting the numerator to the denominator curve produces a canonical univariate weight that carries its asymptotic effect. Eventually, both the defect and the uniform discrepancy in the Forgács–Tran angular parameter are bounded linearly in the weight's degree, yielding a numerator-independent limiting zero distribution. On bulk subarcs away from its zeros the weight gives the numerator-dependent clock correction, while on uniquely dominant branches its zeros attract finite packets of coefficient zeros.
