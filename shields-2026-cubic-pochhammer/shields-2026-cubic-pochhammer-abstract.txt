Title
-----
The cubic threshold for coefficientwise log-concavity of multiple-Pochhammer series

Keywords
--------
coefficientwise log-concavity
coefficientwise Schur-concavity
multiple-Pochhammer series
multiplicity threshold
Turán inequality
beta-binomial distribution
Bernstein basis

Abstract (Plain)
----------------
Multiple-Pochhammer power series associated with a nonnegative log-concave sequence with no internal zeros were shown by Karp and Zhang to be coefficientwise log-concave at multiplicity two, while the cubic case remained open. We prove the cubic conjecture, in the equivalent fixed-sum form of coefficientwise Schur-concavity. Together with a degree-three obstruction for every larger multiplicity, this yields the sharp classification: universal coefficientwise log-concavity holds precisely at multiplicities two and three. Degreewise, the coefficient inequalities are equivalent to monotonicity of a symmetric residue kernel weighted by the anti-diagonal products of the sequence, while log-concavity with no internal zeros is exactly central increase of these weights in every degree. At multiplicity three we prove this kernel monotonicity for every nonnegative centrally increasing symmetric weight sequence.
