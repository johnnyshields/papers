# Lean 4 formalization — Corner descent and repeated inners in skew Schur compositions

The Lean 4.34.1 project `SkewCornerDescent` formalizes generator-index span, canonical connected-diagram Jacobi--Trudi functions, integral component Schur products, southeast-interior component bounds, and local matrix and derivative steps in `shields-2026-skew-corner-descent.tex`. It proves the geometric corner expansion with an integral lower-generator remainder, primitivity of every skew Schur polynomial, connected-diagram irreducibility over every field, and well-defined interior cofactors on connected Schur classes. Copied material is marked `TODO-COPIED` in the source. The tree contains no `sorry` or project axiom.

The paper proves corner transport and discrete descent for skew Schur compositions, and determinant detection and literal-root reconstruction under complete-window, residue, and support hypotheses. Its intrinsic-order alternative detects the original determinant defect within the stated bound or produces a proper whole-diagram literal root. The pure primitive theorem gives first-normal detection or an actual smaller empty root. The bounded ordinary-gauge classification identifies the positive-width pairs through eight cells and their failures of Hypothesis V.

The proofs in the paper are noncomputational and do not depend on the code here.
