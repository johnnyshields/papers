# Lean 4 formalization — Skew Schur equivalence by Jacobi–Trudi negative count

A Lean 4 (`v4.34.1`) development in namespace `SkewJacobiNegative` for `shields-2026-skew-jacobi-negative.tex`.  The compiled complete-generator and beta-diagram producer core is sorry-free and uses no project axioms or `native_decide`.  Copied source units retain explicit provenance comments and their source-bound manuscript references.

The paper classifies connected skew diagrams with at most six full-board negative indices against arbitrary Schur-equal diagrams, allowing common beta entries.  It also proves the sharp nine-negative rigidity threshold at minimum realizing row count at least seven and the two exceptional seven-row families at ten negatives and minimum row count seven.

The proofs in the paper are noncomputational and do not depend on the code here.

## Differences from the paper

The complete-generator representation is related to the copied integral determinant by faithful coefficient extension.  Connected irreducibility is proved over the rational ring by condensation first, then reflected to the integral determinant through its coefficient-one monomial.

The minimum-six ribbon equality is proved by finite symbolic Jacobi–Trudi expansion over an arbitrary commutative ring, rather than by importing the cited ribbon factor-reversal theorem.

The nine-negative six-row equality is proved as a polynomial identity over an arbitrary commutative ring, without localizing at its constant Toeplitz coefficient.  The actual matrix bridge retains the unit boundary.

The six-negative minimum's distinctness follows by proving that the only self-conjugate partition of area six is the staircase, whose minimum order is five, rather than listing all partitions in the minimum-six stratum.

Five-row constant-gap necessity is proved directly from exact interval-coarsening inventories.  Row and edge moments recover the two endpoints; six middle permutations then reduce to explicit edge and cut identities.  This replaces an appeal to the general ribbon factor-reversal classification and retains all coefficient multiplicities and numerical collisions.

Four-row quadratic exchanges are read by largest-index differentiation and the coordinate-times-binomial classification, rather than by specialized-factor degrees.  At an isolated first interval contact, the same classification recovers the last row from the exchange defect instead of reading its private largest generator.
